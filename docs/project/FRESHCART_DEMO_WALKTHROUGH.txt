6. Buttons never to click (they call Claude again): "Go to AI Intake" then "Re-Analyze", "AI Validate", "Re-check (uses AI)", "Find what changed" a second time, and anything in the "NirnAIn AI" chat panel. 7. If something hangs:
  ◦ Safe to reload: Dashboard, Scope Review, WBS Builder, Constraints, Optimization Results and Changes & Versions. Everything is saved as you go; a running AI job or solver run continues on the server and the page picks it up again.

  ◦ If a wait goes past twice the times in Part C, switch to the backup tab and continue from the same step there.
